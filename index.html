Dr. Rajendra Gode College of Pharmacy, Malkapur (Autonomous)

Established in 2005, Dr. Rajendra Gode College of Pharmacy, Malkapur, is committed to excellence in pharmaceutical education, research, innovation, and skill development.

Programs and Research Facilities Offered:

Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm)

Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm)

Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm) with specializations:

Pharmaceutics – 15 seats, Quality Assurance – 15 seats, Pharmaceutical Chemistry – 15 seats

Ph.D. Research Laboratory, 3D Printing Laboratory
